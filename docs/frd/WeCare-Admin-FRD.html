1. Purpose and Objectives

This document defines the functional requirements of the WeCare Admin system. It provides the agreed business behavior required for employee and dependent administration, insurance configuration, claim approval, financial processing, reporting, auditing, notification, support and integration with the WeCare Care App.

The objectives of WeCare Admin are to:

  • Provide secure, role-based administration for multiple companies.
  • Maintain employee, application-user and dependent information.
  • Configure insurance policies, periods, treatment categories and employee limits.
  • Process medical and death-related claims using controlled approval workflows.
  • Maintain accurate policy balances across staff-category changes and policy periods.
  • Generate operational, claim, payment and management reports.
  • Exchange correct status, policy, balance and notification information with the Care App.
  • Provide traceable audit records for Admin and Care App activities.

2. Scope

2.1 In Scope

  • Admin authentication, password reset and role/page/task authorization.
  • System-user, role, privilege, company and reference-data administration.
  • Employee master data, application-user administration, activation/inactivation, civil-status processing and staff-category transfer/promotion.
  • Dependent review, update, approval and rejection.
  • Insurance policy, period, treatment, category, quarter and limit configuration.
  • Indoor, outdoor, spectacle, critical-illness, DDF and death-related claim administration.
  • Multi-level approval, full approval, partial approval, rejection and reason handling.
  • SMS and email notifications currently implemented by the system.
  • Payment attachments, payment advice and financial reporting.
  • Dashboards, operational reports, exports and migrated-data validation.
  • Linode Object Storage-backed document management with API-compatible Base64 responses.
  • Admin, Care App and combined audit activity pages.
  • Support tickets for WeCare Admin and Care App issues.

2.2 Out of Scope

  • Detailed Care App user-interface requirements, except Admin-to-App integration outcomes.
  • Direct Care App user-to-support ticket creation; Care App users contact their Care Admin/HR team.
  • Developer assignment within support tickets.
  • Security-alert emails that are not currently implemented. User creation and OTP/password reset messages remain SMS-based.
  • Infrastructure procurement and third-party service commercial agreements.

4. System Overview

WeCare Admin is the administrative and approval component of the WeCare ecosystem. It exchanges employee, dependent, policy, limit, claim, payment, document and notification information with Care App services and shared database structures.

Reference Configuration → Employee & Dependent Administration → Claim Submission → L1/L2/L3 Review → Final Decision → Balance/Notification → Payment → Reporting & Audit

Admin business APIs use POST operations for reference data, searching/filtering, viewing and state-changing operations, in line with the established system convention. Each protected function is authorized using page and task privileges.

BR-COM-003Reference-data responses shall include the privileges required by the page so the client can enable only authorized actions.

BR-COM-005State-changing operations shall be auditable with actor, date/time, source, action and business context.

BR-COM-008Failures from document, message, email and report services shall be logged and diagnosable without silently changing the business outcome.

7. Key Business Workflows

7.1 Employee Onboarding

  1. HR/Admin selects an assigned company and enters employee master information.
  2. The system validates mandatory fields, NIC and active EPF/company duplication.
  3. Employee personal, address and company records are created.
  4. Where required, employee inclusion is routed for Admin approval and configured recipients are notified.
  5. An application-user account is created/activated according to onboarding rules.
  6. Care App login, profile, company, staff category, transfer date, policy and balance become available.

7.2 Dependent Approval

  1. Employee submits dependent details through Care App.
  2. Same-company HR recipients receive the configured submission email.
  3. HR reviews identity, relation, age, facility, live status and documents.
  4. HR approves or rejects with date, user and remark.
  5. Approval is notified to configured Admin recipients; rejection follows the existing SMS route.
  6. Care App displays the final status and claim eligibility is updated.

7.3 Medical Claim Approval

  1. Care App creates a claim and assigns a unique request ID.
  2. Admin loads the claim with employee/dependent, policy, limit, documents and workflow information.
  3. Level 01 validates and approves, partially recommends or rejects with reasons.
  4. Level 02 performs the next decision and Level 03 is used where escalation rules require it.
  5. The final status is determined as full approval, partial approval or full rejection.
  6. Only the approved amount reduces balance.
  7. Care App status/history and the correctly formatted SMS/message are updated.
  8. Finalized claims proceed to payment attachment, advice and reporting.

7.4 Transfer/Promotion and Historical Policy

  1. Admin selects the employee, new staff category and effective transfer date.
  2. The system records the previous category and current category.
  3. Claims before the effective date use the previous category and previous policy limit.
  4. Claims on/after the effective date use the current category and current policy limit.
  5. Changing current staff category shall not overwrite the historical policy’s category or limit.
  6. Care App year selection shall show the matching historical/current policy and balance.

7.5 Support Ticket

  1. Care Admin user creates either a WeCare Admin or WeCare App issue ticket.
  2. Configured central support recipients receive the creation email.
  3. Client/support users reply and update status within their privileges.
  4. Update notifications reach the opposite side while excluding the action user.
  5. Resolution is recorded and the ticket creator is notified.
  6. A resolved/closed ticket may be reopened according to allowed transitions, notifying support.

9. Data and Migration Requirements

  • All imports shall use controlled templates and explicit company context.
  • Import validation shall occur before transaction commit.
  • Migration shall preserve legacy identifiers where needed for reconciliation.
  • Duplicate detection shall distinguish same-company conflicts from valid cross-company EPF reuse.
  • Historical staff category, policy, limit and claim date context shall not be overwritten by current employment data.
  • Migration output shall list inserted, skipped, failed and conflicting records with row-level reasons.
  • QA shall validate source-to-target counts and representative employee/dependent/claim records before sign-off.

11. Acceptance and UAT Criteria

The WeCare Admin release is acceptable when:

  • All critical Admin smoke tests pass.
  • All Must requirements have corresponding passed QA cases or approved exceptions.
  • No unresolved Critical or High defects remain.
  • Company segregation is demonstrated using at least two companies and a global/unassigned user.
  • Employee onboarding, dependent approval, full approval, partial approval, full rejection, transfer/promotion, document, payment and reporting end-to-end scenarios pass.
  • Historical staff-category policy limits are correct before and after transfer.
  • Active email events and recipient scopes are verified against the actual mail server; SMS-only events remain unchanged.
  • Object Storage upload/retrieval works and configured database binary retention is verified.
  • Migrated record counts and samples reconcile with source data.
  • Function-wise UAT and overall business-flow UAT are signed off.

13. Assumptions, Constraints and Exclusions

  • Company, role, approval-level, policy and treatment master data will be configured before dependent and claim testing.
  • Recipient rules are only effective when both the event and rule are ACTIVE and recipient users have usable email addresses.
  • SAME_COMPANY_OR_UNASSIGNED includes users assigned to the transaction company and global users with no company assignment; ALL_COMPANIES ignores company assignment.
  • Support-ticket emails are newly introduced behavior; other migrated email workflows retain existing templates and business triggers.
  • Actual email/SMS delivery depends on external provider availability and valid environment configuration.
  • Production migration and deployment require approved backup, rollback, smoke-test and sign-off procedures.
  • Where the current implementation and an approved signed requirement differ, the approved change request shall control the next release.
